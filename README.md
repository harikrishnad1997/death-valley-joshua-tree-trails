# Death Valley + Joshua Tree Trip

GitHub Pages site for the Dec 17–23, 2026 Death Valley + Joshua Tree trip: every trail link (AllTrails + Google Maps), grouped by day.

- **28 stops in the plan, all with a verified AllTrails trail page**, plus Ubehebe Crater as an optional Sunday detour, Travelers Monument as an optional Monday stop, and Castle Mountains as an optional Monday detour (instead of Teutonia Peak) — 34 map pins in total: the 30 park-day pins plus 3 Friday stops (Lake Mead VC, Tule Springs Aliante, Alien Center) on their own map layer, plus the Castle Mountains pin.
- One table per day (Fri Dec 18 – Wed Dec 23): stop, AllTrails distance, hike time, ideal start time, parking lot, and AllTrails + Google Maps links, with a short description of each stop underneath. The visitor center stop is a row in each day's table (Furnace Creek Sat, Stovepipe Wells backup Sun, Hole-in-the-Wall Mon, Joshua Tree Tue). Sunrise/sunset rows also carry the sunrise/sunset clock time in the stop name.
- Google Maps links use exact trailhead coordinates (Nov 14 fix — the name-based links resolved to the wrong places for several trailheads, e.g. Desolation Canyon, Kelso Dunes, Amboy Crater).
- **Fri Dec 18 is a Las Vegas afternoon** (work ends 2 PM PT): Lake Mead Visitor Center (Lake Mead + Tule Springs passport stamps) and the Tule Springs Aliante trailhead, then the 9 PM rental pickup and the night drive to Furnace Creek via US-95, with the Area 51 Alien Center as a conditional stop (closes 11 PM). The evening adds dinner downtown, the Neon Museum, and Fremont Street before the pickup.
- **Mon Dec 21 is a full Mojave National Preserve day** on the transfer between the parks: Teutonia Peak, Kelso Dunes, Hole-in-the-Wall Rings Trail (passport stamps for Mojave + Castle Mountains), Lava Tube, and Amboy Crater as a viewpoint stop — plus an optional first detour to the Travelers Monument on Soda Lake (dry lakebed only), and Castle Mountains as an optional detour instead of Teutonia Peak (~3.5–4 hr round trip). The full Amboy loop doesn't fit the day.
- **Visitor centers section** — every center the trip needs, with hours and a Food inside? column (only the downtown Joshua Tree center has a café inside): Lake Mead Visitor Center (Lake Mead + Tule Springs stamps), Furnace Creek (Death Valley stamp), Stovepipe Wells Ranger Station (backup stamp), Hole-in-the-Wall (Mojave + Castle Mountains stamps), Joshua Tree Visitor Center and the Twentynine Palms center (Joshua Tree stamp).
- **Packing checklist** — hiking gear (trekking poles included), December-desert clothing, water/food, tech, passport book and regional sticker sets, plus a food-to-pack group: bread, peanut butter + jam, trail mix, granola bars, fruit, coffee, Coke Zero.
- **Places to eat** — vegetarian options at each stop: Las Vegas, Death Valley's resort restaurants, Baker, Joshua Tree town, and Pioneertown.
- **Food to pack on the way** — Panera in Las Vegas, Albertsons and Walmart in Pahrump, Stater Bros in Yucca Valley, for sandwiches and trail-food supplies.
- **Trip map** (`map.html`): Leaflet map with the stops numbered in visit order, color-coded by day, with a day filter. Each day's section embeds its own filtered map (`map.html?day=day0` … `day5`, only that day's pins) with a full-screen link; the full map stays embedded at the bottom and at `map.html`. Map popup links also use coordinates.
- **Live weather** for Furnace Creek and Twentynine Palms (Open-Meteo), with the Dec 19–23 trip days highlighted.
- No personal information on the public page (no booking confirmations, costs, or passenger names; flight numbers and tracking links were added Nov 15 at the user's request).

Distances and hike times are AllTrails' figures unless noted — where AllTrails lists no hike time, the cell gives the best available figure and tags its source (mirror = AllTrails data on another site, NPS = park service, guides = hiking guides). Start times are estimates built from the hike times and the day's anchors (sunrise/sunset, visitor center hours). Trail list source: `workspace/goals/death-valley-and-joshua-tree-trip/files/trail-links.json`.

Site files: `index.md` (the page), `map.html` (the Leaflet map), `_config.yml` (Cayman theme).
